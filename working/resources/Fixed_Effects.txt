Yes — the cleanest way to think about fixed effects is:

> **Each specification answers a different causal question because it keeps different variation.**

Here is a summary table.

| Specification                                                       | What variation identifies the effect?                                                                  | What question are you answering?                                                                                                                                                                                                  | What are you *not* answering?                                                                    | Main risk / interpretation issue                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Honduras only, no FE**                                            | Differences across municipalities and across years                                                     | “Are more-exposed Honduran municipalities darker after Mitch?”                                                                                                                                                                    | Not a credible causal question yet                                                               | Confounds exposure with permanent municipal differences and national time shocks                                                          |
| **Honduras only + municipality FE + year FE**                       | **Within-Honduras, across municipalities**, before vs after Mitch                                      | **“Within Honduras, after netting out each municipality’s baseline level and each year’s national shock, did more-exposed municipalities experience larger post-Mitch losses in night lights than less-exposed municipalities?”** | You are **not** estimating the average national effect of Mitch on Honduras as a whole           | If Mitch hit almost all of Honduras, year FE absorb the common national hit; you only estimate the **relative** local gradient            |
| **Honduras only + municipality FE + year FE + municipality trends** | Same as above, but now deviations from municipality-specific trends                                    | “Did more-exposed municipalities deviate from their own prior trend more than less-exposed municipalities after Mitch?”                                                                                                           | Still not the national average loss                                                              | Can be more convincing if pre-trends differ, but may over-control and wash out real effects if the panel is short ([deepeconomics.in][1]) |
| **Regional pooled + municipality FE + year FE**                     | Across municipalities in all countries, before vs after, with only common calendar-year shocks removed | **“Across the region, did more-exposed municipalities experience larger post-Mitch losses than less-exposed municipalities, after netting out municipal baselines and shocks common to all countries in a given year?”**          | Not a clean within-country question                                                              | Country-specific post-1998 macro divergence can contaminate the estimate                                                                  |
| **Regional pooled + municipality FE + country FE + year FE**        | Same as above, but permanent country differences are removed                                           | “Across the region, net of permanent municipal and country differences and common year shocks, did more-exposed municipalities do worse after Mitch?”                                                                             | Still not a clean within-country question                                                        | Better than plain year FE, but still leaves country-specific post-1998 paths uncontrolled                                                 |
| **Regional pooled + municipality FE + country-year FE**             | **Within each country-year, across municipalities with different exposure**                            | **“Within each country in each year, did more-exposed municipalities experience larger post-Mitch losses in night lights than less-exposed municipalities in that same country-year?”**                                           | You are **not** estimating the average effect of Mitch on Honduras, Nicaragua, etc. as countries | This is the cleanest specification for a **local differential effect**, but it removes all country-level average disaster effects         |
| **Country-level panel (country FE + year FE)**                      | Across countries, before vs after Mitch                                                                | **“Did the most Mitch-exposed countries experience a worse post-1998 path than less-exposed countries?”**                                                                                                                         | Not a municipal/local effect                                                                     | Very few countries; hard to separate Mitch from broader country-specific structural differences                                           |
| **Synthetic-control / country counterfactual for Honduras**         | Honduras vs a constructed comparison path                                                              | **“How did Honduras as a whole perform after Mitch relative to a counterfactual Honduras without Mitch?”**                                                                                                                        | Not a municipal exposure-gradient question                                                       | Better aligned with the ‘50 years lost’ rhetoric, but a different project than your municipality DiD                                      |

## The one-line meaning of each main option

If you want the simplest version for your notes:

* **Honduras only + municipality FE + year FE**
  “Among Honduran municipalities, did harder-hit places do worse than less-hit places after Mitch?”

* **Regional pooled + municipality FE + year FE**
  “Across Central American municipalities, did harder-hit places do worse after Mitch?”
  But this may partly reflect country-level divergence.

* **Regional pooled + municipality FE + country-year FE**
  “Within each country-year, did harder-hit municipalities do worse than less-hit municipalities?”
  This is the cleanest **local** question.

* **Country-level counterfactual approach**
  “How much did Honduras as a country lose because of Mitch?”
  This is the cleanest **national-loss** question.
